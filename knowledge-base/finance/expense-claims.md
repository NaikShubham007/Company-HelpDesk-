# Expense Claims and Reimbursement

Submit business expense claims within 30 days of the expense date, through the finance portal.

## Receipts

Attach an itemised receipt to every claim above 500 INR. Claims without a required receipt are rejected.

## Travel limits

Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Hotel costs are capped at 6,000 INR per night in metro cities and 4,500 INR per night elsewhere.

## Approval and payment

Claims up to 10,000 INR are approved by your manager. Claims above 10,000 INR also need approval from the Finance team. Approved claims are paid in the next payroll cycle.
