# Annual and Sick Leave Policy

## Annual leave entitlement

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees who join after 1 July receive a pro-rated amount.

## Requesting leave

Request leave in the HR portal. Ask at least 10 working days in advance for leave longer than 3 days. Your manager must approve the request.

## Carrying leave forward

Up to 5 unused days can be carried into the next year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from annual leave and gives 10 days per year. Notify your manager before your shift starts. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
