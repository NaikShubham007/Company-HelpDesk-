# Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. During core hours you must be reachable on chat and in video calls.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed 3 months with the company are eligible. New joiners work from the office for their first month unless their manager agrees otherwise.

## Equipment and workspace

A company laptop is required. The home office stipend of 1,500 INR per year covers a chair, a desk, and internet top-ups.

## Security at home

Connect through the company VPN or use the managed laptop for all work. Do not print confidential documents at home.
