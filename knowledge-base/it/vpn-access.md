# VPN Access and Setup

Employees need the company VPN to reach internal systems from home or while travelling.

## Who can use the VPN

All full-time employees and approved contractors. Access is enabled for employees after their first day.

## How to install

1. Open the Software Center on your laptop and install "Company VPN Client".
2. Launch the client and enter your work email as the username.
3. Approve the sign-in on your phone with the authenticator app.

## Connection rules

- Connect the VPN before opening any internal application.
- The VPN disconnects automatically after 12 hours of inactivity.
- The VPN is for work traffic only. Do not use it for personal streaming.

## Troubleshooting

If the client shows "Authentication failed", check that your password is current (see the Password Reset policy) and try again. If it still fails after three attempts, raise a ticket with IT Service Desk. Include your laptop name and the time of the error.
