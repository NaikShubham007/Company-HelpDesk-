# Password Reset and Account Lockout

## Self-service reset

Use the self-service portal from the sign-in page by choosing "Forgot password". You need access to your registered mobile phone. The new password takes effect within 15 minutes.

## Password rules

- At least 14 characters, with upper and lower case letters, a number, and a symbol.
- Passwords expire every 90 days.
- You cannot reuse your last 10 passwords.
- Never share your password. IT staff will never ask for it.

## Locked accounts

An account locks after 5 failed sign-in attempts and unlocks automatically after 30 minutes. For an immediate unlock, contact IT Service Desk.
