# Laptop and Equipment Requests

Every new employee receives a standard laptop on their first day. Request other equipment, such as monitors, keyboards, or docking stations, through the IT request form.

## Standard laptop

Each employee gets one standard laptop. It is replaced every 4 years, or sooner if it is faulty.

## Requesting extra equipment

- Submit the IT request form with your manager's approval.
- Monitors and docking stations are approved for hybrid and remote staff.
- Standard requests are fulfilled within 5 working days.

## Lost or damaged devices

Report a lost or stolen device to IT Service Desk within 24 hours. Damaged devices are repaired under warranty for up to 2 incidents per year. After that, the repair cost may be charged to your department.
